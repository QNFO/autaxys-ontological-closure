## Abstract
Operational criterion for physical reality: a quantity is physically real only if there exists a finite Turing-machine protocol that approximates it with a computable modulus of convergence, yielding measurement-distinguishable predictions. The criterion admits standard physical quantities (mass, charge, spin, energy levels) and excludes uncomputable constants, non-measurable spectral projections, and continuum fields at arbitrarily small scales. Decomposes into 11 testable sub-claims across quantum foundations, cosmology, thermodynamics, information theory, and computability theory.
